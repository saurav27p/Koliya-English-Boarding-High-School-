Our school

A place to begin
with purpose.

Koliya English Boarding High School is based in Devdaha-10, Charange, Rupandehi. Established in 2057 BS, the school serves students from PG through Class 10 with a guiding belief captured in four words: education for all.

The school brings together more than thirty teachers and offers a learning environment designed to support students across the full span of their early education.
